A Little About Me & Why I StartedElite Labs & IV ❤️

I’ve been doing blood draws for 10 years, working in ERs, hospitals, doctor’s offices, and labs. Throughout my career, I’ve often been the go-to person for hard sticks and difficult draws.

But for me, it’s always been about more than just getting the blood. I truly love meeting new people and making the experience as quick, easy, comfortable, and stress-free as possible.

I started Elite Labs & IV because I wanted to bring that same experience and compassionate care directly to YOU.

Instead of traveling to a busy lab, I come to your home, assisted living community, or wherever you’re most comfortable. This makes getting labs done easier for elderly patients, people with mobility issues, parents, caregivers, and anyone who has difficulty getting around.

I truly love what I do, and I’m so grateful for every person who trusts me with their care. 💙
